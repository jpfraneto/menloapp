First, [set up Menlo on your iPhone](/guide/start/install-and-onboard/#start-from-scratch) and configure a coding agent on your Mac.

## Write the intention

Open the creation composer on Mac or send an intent from Menlo on iPhone. Describe the main action and the result:

> Make a breathing timer. The circle expands for four seconds and contracts for six. Tap to start or pause. Remember the last duration.

You can supply an app name and up to eight PNG/JPEG references. Keep credentials and private information out of references.

## Send once

On Mac, **Return** sends. **Shift–Return** adds a line.

Your Mac implements, builds, signs, and delivers the app to your intended iPhone. If a verified build is waiting for the phone, it stays **Ready to install** and resumes when the phone returns.

Creation stays private until you [share the app](/guide/start/share-an-app/).
