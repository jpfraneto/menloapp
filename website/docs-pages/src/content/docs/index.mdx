## Share in two commands

From your committed, pushed public GitHub app folder:

```sh
npm i -g menloapp
menloapp deploy
```

Deploy returns your app link and an exact-version link for sharing. It creates missing listing metadata and can record a Simulator preview. [Customize your app page](/guide/product/app-listing/).

## One app link. Specific versions.

The ordinary link follows your repository’s default branch. An exact-version link keeps the commit and repository identity you shared, even after another push. Recipients choose when to update; a newer commit does not silently replace the selected version or the installed app.

[Learn about source reviews](/guide/security/source-reviews/): a signed-in GitHub user can recommend one commit and build recipe. Reviews never carry over to new source and never replace your own build consent.

## What happens on your Mac

The website is the listing and review surface. Your Mac retrieves the selected source, runs Xcode, and uses your Apple signing identity. Your intended iPhone receives the result after Trust, Developer Mode, and installation checks. Trying a linked app does not require installing Menlo on the iPhone first.
